# 08 · Decisions, open questions, out of scope

## Locked decisions (from the grill session, 14/11/2026)
| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| 1 | Name: **Merc** |
| 2 | Customer: mid-market B2B with 20–200 field reps, field-first industries |
| 3 | We replace the customer's CRM (our own CRM), we don't do an overlay |
| 4 | Buyer = CEO, users = reps |
| 5 | Greece first, Greek and English |
| 6 | Mobile + voice for the rep, desktop for management |
| 7 | MVP: voice logging, briefing + cards, prospecting, alerts (all in parallel) |
| 8 | Management in the MVP: dashboard, territories & assignments, campaigns |
| 9 | New shops from business directories, company data, customer lists. Research by web scraping the sites. No scraping of maps |
| 10 | AI autonomy: internal automatic with undo, external with approval, configurable per action and role |
| 11 | Connections: email, calendar, WhatsApp/Viber, ERP (file in the MVP) |
| 12 | Migration: AI import from Excel/CSV |
| 13 | Offline recording + sync |
| 14 | Pricing: per rep, base plan with agents/voice, add-ons per agent/feature |
| 15 | Voice buddy on Wonderful (separate dev). Everything else is built by the technical team |
