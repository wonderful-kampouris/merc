## J2 · Manager (Eleni, 12 reps)
1. Monday morning: team dashboard → 2 reps with low logging, 7 at-risk customers in the North territory.
2. Creates a campaign "New espresso coffee → cafés, North + Center, 1 week".
3. On Tuesday the campaign appears in the briefings and on the café cards.
4. On Friday she sees: shown 40 times, pitched 22, orders 6.
5. A rep leaves → bulk transfer of their customers to someone else, with all the history.

## J3 · CEO (buyer)
1. Demo: sees J1 live + dashboard.
2. Onboarding: uploads the customers and orders Excel → AI import → in a few minutes the CRM is full.
3. Settings: defines the ICP, the briefing time, and that emails always need approval.
4. Monthly report: visits ↑, CRM completeness, new customers, customers saved.

## J4 · Prospecting (rep or manager)
"Bakeries, Chalandri" → 18 candidates → 5 already exist and are excluded → 13 with a dossier and score → the top 4 are added to the week's routes → after the visit, "became a customer" (with approval).
