# 01 · Vision & Positioning

## In one sentence
**Merc is the CRM that fills itself:** every field rep has an AI buddy that talks to them, knows every customer, researches every new shop, does the paperwork, and asks for approval only for what the customer will see.

## The problem
| Problem | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Field reps spend most of their week on notes, data entry, emails, prep and driving | Fewer visits, fewer sales |
| CRMs were built for the office and the keyboard | In the field they stay empty or out of date |
| Management can't see what's happening in the market | Decisions made blind, competition spotted late |
| Knowledge stays in the rep's head | When the rep leaves, relationships and history leave with them |
| Customers "fade away" slowly | Lost revenue that nobody noticed in time |

## Target customer (ICP)
| | |
|---|---|
| Company type | Mid-market B2B with **20–200 field reps** |
| Industries | Distributors and brands in food/beverage/FMCG (HoReCa, mini markets, bakeries), building/electrical materials, agri-inputs, pharma, telecom/energy |
| Geography | Greece first, in Greek and English. Then non-English-speaking markets |
| Current state | They have an old, expensive or empty CRM, or Excel/ERP/WhatsApp |

## Who buys, who uses
| Role | What they want | What we "sell" them |
|---|---|---|
| **CEO (buyer)** | Growth, visibility, not losing customers | One dashboard with the truth about the market, more visits per rep, new customers, early warning on at-risk customers |
| **Sales manager** | To know what the team is doing, to coach | Live team view, territories, campaigns |
| **Rep (user)** | Less paperwork, more sales | "You talk, you don't type." Always knows who to see and what to say |

## Value proposition
1. **Zero data entry:** 40 seconds of voice after the visit and the CRM is up to date.
2. **Always prepared:** a morning briefing and a card for every stop, with a pitch for new customers.
3. **New customers without searching:** Merc finds, researches and scores new shops in the rep's area.
4. **Nothing gets forgotten:** alerts for customers who went quiet, deals that got stuck and promises that are due.
5. **The human is in control:** the AI does internal things on its own. Anything the customer sees goes through approval.
6. **The knowledge stays with the company:** each customer's memory belongs to the company, not to the rep.

## Position against the competition
| Player | What they do | Why we win |
|---|---|---|
| Salesforce / big CRMs | Powerful but expensive (AI as an add-on on top of an already expensive license), built for the office, hard to use | AI-native from the start, voice in Greek, fast setup, built for the field |
| HubSpot / Pipedrive | Easy, but inside-sales | Built for visits, routes and voice |
| Field tools (route/maps apps) | Routes, check-ins | A full CRM + agents, not just a map |
| Excel / ERP / WhatsApp | Free and familiar | The same ease (you talk), but with memory, alerts and a view for management |

**Position:** *Merc replaces the CRM, it doesn't sit on top of it.* Switching is made easy with AI import (see 03).

## Revenue model (direction)
| Element | Logic |
|---|---|
| **Base plan per rep/month** | CRM, voice logging, briefing, visit cards, alerts, dashboard |
| **Add-ons per agent/feature** | Prospecting, outbound calls/voice campaigns, WhatsApp/Viber messages, advanced analytics |
| **Usage credits (suggestion)** | Voice minutes and research above the plan's limit |
| Market benchmark | AI add-on of a big CRM ~$125/user/month on top of the license. Inside-sales CRM ~$90–150/seat. Field tools ~$50–150/user |
⚠️ Final prices are open and depend on the voice/AI cost per rep.
