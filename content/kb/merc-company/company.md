# Delta Foods & Coffee: who we are and how we sell

This is the company profile Merc uses to frame its hints. It explains *why* the push and avoid rules in `product_guidance` exist. Prices, stock dates and quantities never come from this document. They come from the tables.

## Who we are
- A family-run Greek distributor of coffee, beverages, bakery supplies and consumables for cafés, bakeries, restaurants and mini markets.
- We roast our own espresso blends and deliver with our own vans, so we control quality and delivery days.
- We win on reliability and on the relationship with the owner. We do not try to be the cheapest supplier.

## Our values
1. **Fix before you sell.** A customer with an open problem gets a fix and a date before any new product.
2. **Honest promises.** Never promise a delivery, a price or a discount we cannot keep. If stock is short, say so.
3. **One supplier, less hassle.** We sell the whole counter: coffee, milk, syrups, cups. One delivery, one invoice.
4. **Quality over volume.** We protect the premium position of our espresso. We would rather lose a low-margin line than cheapen the brand.
5. **Respect the owner's time.** Short visits between peak hours, one clear next step, no pressure.

## How we sell
- Lead with the shop, not the catalog: their menu, their customers, their last order.
- Add at most one new product per visit. Campaign and product-of-the-month items come first.
- Prefer add-ons that pair with what the customer already buys (plant milk or syrups with espresso).
- Trial before commitment: a trial bag or a sample beats a discount.

## What we never do
- Push a product that is out of stock or cannot be delivered on the promised day.
- Sell instant or budget lines into a specialty café that buys our premium espresso. It undercuts the story we sold them.
- Push low-margin consumables to customers who did not ask for them.
- Talk badly about a competitor. We compare on facts the customer can check.

## How Merc should use this
- Frame the push product's reason in terms of these values ("fixes your grinder problem", "one delivery").
- Explain the avoid product's reason in one short sentence the rep can act on.
- Never invent a fact, number or product that is not in the brief's input.
