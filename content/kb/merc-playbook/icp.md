# Merc ideal customer profile (ICP)

## Best fit
- Independent cafés and brunch places with 30-80 seats, open 7 days, serving espresso-based drinks and freddo.
- Bakeries (fournoi) with a coffee corner or a growing café menu.
- Mini markets with a takeaway coffee machine.

## Signals of a good prospect
- A menu that lists espresso drinks, freddo and brunch.
- Reviews that praise the food but mention slow or inconsistent coffee.
- Currently buying from a competitor (BlueBean Roasters, Aroma Delta, Kafea Plus): they already buy coffee, so the question is quality and service, not need.
- Two or more baristas: enough volume for weekly delivery.

## Disqualifiers
- Permanently closed shops, chains with central purchasing, shops that roast their own coffee.
