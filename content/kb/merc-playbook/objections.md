# Merc objection library

## "We are happy with BlueBean Roasters."
Answer: BlueBean is a good roaster, so we do not ask you to switch blindly. Try a 1 kg trial bag of Espresso Nuovo side by side for a week; it sits in the same flavour range, so the baristas need only a small grind change. If your customers do not notice the difference, you lose nothing.

## "Aroma Delta offered us 15% lower."
Answer: Ask what is included: delivery frequency, machine service, consumables. Our price includes weekly delivery and grinder service visits. If a price gap remains, do not match on the spot: tell them you will check with your manager (discount approval tiers apply).

## "Kafea Plus is cheaper."
Answer: Compare cost per cup, not per kilo: a blend that is forgiving on the grinder wastes fewer shots. Offer a trial before talking price.

## "It is too expensive."
Answer: Break it down per cup (about 7 g per espresso, so roughly 140 cups per kg). Ten cents more per kilo is a fraction of a cent per cup, while a better cup sells more freddo.

## "I have no time now."
Answer: Respect it. Leave the trial bag and ask for a 5-minute slot outside peak hours (after 10:30 or after 14:30).

## "Changing supplier is a hassle."
Answer: We handle the switch: first delivery with the trial, the grind setting written down for the baristas, and the same delivery day every week.

## "Your last delivery was late." / "The grinder is making noise."
Answer: Acknowledge first, then give a concrete fix and a date (a technician visit, a replacement part, a delivery slot). Sell nothing until the problem has a date.
