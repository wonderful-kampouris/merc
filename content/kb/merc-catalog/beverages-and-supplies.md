# Merc catalog: beverages, bakery and supplies

## Beverages
- **Hot Chocolate 1kg** (CHO-1, kg, 12.90 EUR): rich cocoa, winter menu and kids. Pairs with plant milk.
- **Tea Selection 50pcs** (TEA-50, box, 7.90 EUR): six flavours, for cafés that want a full hot-drink menu.
- **Vanilla Syrup 1L** (SYR-VAN, litre, 8.90 EUR) and **Caramel Syrup 1L** (SYR-CAR, litre, 8.90 EUR): flavoured lattes and freddo; raise the average ticket.
- **Plant Milk Barista 1L** (MLK-PLT, litre, 3.20 EUR): oat-based, foams like dairy. Cafés with a young crowd order it every week.

## Bakery ingredients
- **Bread Flour 25kg** (FLR-25, kg, 24.50 EUR), **Dry Yeast 500g** (YST-500, pack, 4.10 EUR), **Pastry Butter 2kg** (BTR-2, kg, 16.80 EUR), **Sesame 1kg** (SES-1, kg, 5.40 EUR): for bakeries and fournoi; reliable weekly delivery is the main selling point.

## Takeaway supplies
- **Paper Cups 8oz x1000** (CUP-8, box, 39.00 EUR) and **Paper Cups 12oz x1000** (CUP-12, box, 46.00 EUR), **Lids x1000** (LID-1000, box, 19.00 EUR), **Sugar Sticks x1000** (SUG-ST, box, 12.50 EUR), **Napkins x2000** (NAP-1, box, 11.00 EUR).
- Why they fit: one supplier for coffee and consumables means one delivery and one invoice. Good add-on whenever a café orders coffee.

## Pairing suggestions
- Coffee order without cups: suggest Paper Cups 8oz and Lids.
- Brunch café: Filter Coffee + Plant Milk Barista + Tea Selection.
- Café with a sweet menu: Espresso Nuovo + Vanilla or Caramel Syrup.
