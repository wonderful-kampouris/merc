# Merc catalog: coffee

## Espresso Classico 1kg (SKU ESP-CL-1)
- Unit: kg bag. List price 18.90 EUR per kg.
- Profile: medium-dark roast, 70% arabica / 30% robusta, chocolate and roasted-nut notes, thick crema.
- Why it fits: the workhorse blend for busy cafés. Forgiving on grinders that are not perfectly calibrated, stable in milk drinks and freddo espresso.
- Typical buyer: neighbourhood cafés pulling 3-6 kg a week. Most café customers order it every week in the same quantity.
- Pairs with: Plant Milk Barista 1L, Paper Cups 8oz, Sugar Sticks.

## Espresso Nuovo Blend 1kg (SKU ESP-NEW-1)
- Unit: kg bag. List price 21.50 EUR per kg.
- Profile: new blend, 90% arabica, lighter roast, caramel and red-fruit notes, sweeter in freddo cappuccino.
- Why it fits: for specialty-leaning cafés that want a premium story on the menu without switching to single origin. Strong answer to customers who praise "smooth" coffee.
- Campaign: the current Espresso Nuovo campaign offers a 1 kg trial bag to cafés.
- Switching from BlueBean Roasters or Kafea Plus: similar flavour range, so baristas need only a small grind adjustment.
- Pairs with: Vanilla Syrup, Caramel Syrup, Plant Milk Barista.
