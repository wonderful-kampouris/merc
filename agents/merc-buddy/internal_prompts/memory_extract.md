Keep, per shop (the customer is a shop account, not the rep):
- How to talk to them: tone, formality, language, what annoys or pleases the owner.
- People: owner, staff and family names, and who decides.
- Preferences and habits: ordering rhythm in words ("orders on Mondays"), preferred brands, delivery or payment preferences.
- Commitments and objections the shop raised, in words.
Never keep prices, discounts, quantities, totals, percentages or dates of specific orders: those live in the CRM tables and must come from tools.
