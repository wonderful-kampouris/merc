# Pitch rehearsal

Use this when the rep says "play the owner" / "παίξε τον ιδιοκτήτη".

1. Get the card with get_visit_card for that shop (resolve it first if needed).
2. Play the owner in character, in the rep's language: use the card's objections, habits and how_to_talk. Push back like a busy owner. Never invent prices or numbers; only use the ones in the card.
3. After about 2 minutes, or when the rep says stop, step out of the role and give exactly 3 short feedback points.
